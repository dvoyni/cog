# Atlases: run-time packing, and how engines auto-atlas

Research for [#863](https://github.com/dvoyni/cog/issues/863), on the map [#860](https://github.com/dvoyni/cog/issues/860). It asks how 2D should pack sprites and glyphs into atlases at run time: the packing algorithms and their cost when images arrive one at a time, one big texture against layers of a 2D-array texture under WebGPU's limits, how a packer reclaims freed space and how engines evict, padding and mips at the edges, what is left out of atlases, and how Unity, Godot, Bevy and the dynamic glyph caches decide what they pack and when. It records facts and decides nothing for 2D. Where a line is reasoning rather than a source's claim, it says so.

## In short

- **For images that arrive one at a time and are freed, the shelf allocator is what the dynamic atlases in production use.** WebRender's shared texture cache and glyphon both use etagere, a shelf allocator that frees each item and merges free neighbours. MaxRects packs best, and skyline is cheapest, but neither implementation read here frees a single rectangle: stb, fontstash and Dear ImGui (skyline) only reset or repack, and TextMesh Pro (MaxRects) only clears. guillotiere frees in constant time, but its author found it slower and more prone to fragmentation than the shelf allocator on WebRender's long sessions.
- **Fill rate depends more on how many pages stay open than on the algorithm.** In Jylänki's online tests, a packer that keeps one bin open uses 39–75% more bins than the best known packing. A packer that may place into any open bin is within 4–12% (MaxRects 1.04, skyline 1.06, shelf with a waste map 1.07, plain shelf 1.12). Offline, with the whole set known and sorted, MaxRects reaches 1.005.
- **WebGPU's defaults allow a 2D-array texture of 256 layers of 8192 × 8192.** One such layer in RGBA8 is 256 MiB, so memory, not the limits, bounds a page. Compatibility mode lowers the side to 4096. A texture's size and layer count are fixed when it is made, so growing an array means a new texture and a copy, or a second array. All pages in one array share one binding, so draws on different pages need not break a batch. Separate textures break it, which is why WebRender works to settle each kind of atlas back to one texture.
- **Engines evict by last use, not by a count of users.** WebRender evicts least-recently-used entries at the start of a frame, never one used in the previous frame, with an age threshold from 400 frames down to 1 as memory pressure rises. glyphon evicts glyphs not used this frame. Dear ImGui drops font sizes unused for 2 frames when full, then repacks into a new texture if at least 20% of the packed area was discarded, or grows the texture. Bevy, Godot and TextMesh Pro never evict glyphs on their own.
- **A full atlas is treated as transient, not as a refusal for good.** Dear ImGui evicts, repacks or grows, then retries, up to four tries. glyphon fails only when every glyph is in use this frame. WebRender compacts, moving up to 512 × 512 texels per frame from the last texture into the first. v1 cached its refusal terminally.
- **Padding covers bilinear filtering; mips need far more.** Padding defaults are 1 (Dear ImGui), 2 (Bevy's glyphs, v1's sprites), 4 (Unity). NVIDIA shows that bilinear sampling of any lower mip pulls in neighbouring texels, and that box-filtering a whole atlas mixes images unless each one is a power of two placed on its own power-of-two lines. render generates a texture's mips by drawing each level from the whole level above, which is that box filter.
- **What stays out:** images larger than a size limit become textures of their own (WebRender above 512 texels; v1's oversized tiled sprites). Repeating images cannot use the sampler's address modes inside an atlas. Engines tile with geometry, as Bevy's `SpriteImageMode::Tiled` does with slices, or emulate wrapping in the shader, as v1 did, which NVIDIA notes needs derivatives.
- **Build time or run time:** Unity's sprite atlases and Godot's `TextureAtlas` import are packed in the editor or at build. Bevy's `TextureAtlasBuilder` packs a known set at run time on request. Only glyph caches, and WebRender's image cache, pack at run time without being asked: TextMesh Pro's dynamic font assets, Godot's text server, Bevy's font atlases, Dear ImGui and fontstash.

## 1. Packing algorithms

| | Shelf | Skyline | Guillotine | MaxRects |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| **Keeps** | rows of fixed height, each filled left to right | the top edge of what is packed, as a list of segments; "lossy": space under an overhang is lost | a list of free rectangles, each split in two on placement | every maximal free rectangle, overlapping |
| **Cost per insert** | walk the shelves | walk the skyline, linear in what is packed | walk the free list | split every overlapping free rectangle, then prune the list in pairs: O(\|F\|²) |
| **Online, one bin open (-BNF)** | 1.52 (2.27) | 1.39 (1.65) | 1.45; best worst case 1.77 | 1.41 (1.79) |
| **Online, any bin open (-BFF/-BBF)** | 1.12; 1.07 with a waste map | 1.06 (1.16) | ≈1.06 | 1.04 (1.13) |
| **Frees one item** | etagere: yes, merging neighbours | no implementation read | guillotiere: yes, merging siblings | no implementation read |
| **Used by** | etagere (WebRender, glyphon), Godot's glyph cache, v1 | stb_rect_pack (Dear ImGui), fontstash | guillotiere (Bevy's `DynamicTextureAtlasBuilder`, WebRender's one-frame atlases) | TextMesh Pro's dynamic fonts |

Scores are Jylänki's: the bins an algorithm used divided by the fewest any algorithm found, averaged over instances of 1000 random rectangles, worst case in brackets ([paper](https://github.com/juj/RectangleBinPack/blob/83e7e1132d93777e3732dfaae26b0f3703be2036/RectangleBinPack.pdf) §4.2, tables 7, 12, 15, 18 and 21).

### Jylänki's survey

- **Online and offline.** "Online" is the problem where rectangles "are received from some input one at a time, and they must immediately be placed … without any knowledge of the upcoming items". Every algorithm in the survey works online: they "place all the rectangles in the order they are given and never move a rectangle that has already been placed". Offline improvements are sorting the input and choosing the globally best placement each step (§1, §3).
- **Choosing the bin.** Under Bin Next Fit (-BNF), "when the next input rectangle does not fit into that bin, that bin is closed and completely forgotten about". Bin First Fit (-BFF) tries the open bins in order, and Best Bin Fit (-BBF) scores them all (§3.1). An atlas whose earlier pages stay open is -BFF or -BBF, which is where the near-1.0 scores are.
- **Shelf.** "The performance of any of the Shelf algorithms is not good enough to recommend the use of these algorithms except when fast runtime performance is needed. In online instances the Shelf algorithms can consume twice the number of bins in the worst case, and about 1.5 times on average" (§4.3). The waste-map variant (-WM) keeps the space above short items in a guillotine structure as a second chance, and is what brings online shelf to 1.07 (§2.1.8, table 7).
- **Guillotine.** Rectangle Merge (-RM) "improve[s] the results in all cases". The best online result with one bin open is GUILLOTINE-MINAS-RM-BNF-BAF at 1.445 (§4.4).
- **MaxRects.** "The best performing algorithms are the MAXRECTS variants": 1.408 (1.788) online with one bin, 1.041 (1.130) with any bin (MAXRECTS-BSSF-BBF), and 1.005 (1.068) offline with the global choice (§4.5). Its cost is the pruning step, O(|F|²n) over n rectangles, and the count of maximal free rectangles can be quadratic in n. "In practice … we have observed that the size of [F] is linear in n", suggesting O(n³) on average (§2.4.5, propositions 8 and 11).
- **Skyline.** It was proposed as "a simplified data structure" for bottom-left placement that "produce[s] packings a lot faster than the ones using the Maximal Rectangles data structure", at a little worse fill (§2.5). It was the best online packer with one bin open, SKYLINE-BL-WM-BNF at 1.392 (1.654) (§4.6).
- **Conclusion.** "The MAXRECTS algorithms perform the best of all. The SKYLINE algorithms performed the best when packing is performed online only to a single bin at a time (-BNF). The GUILLOTINE variants are asymptotically faster than the MAXRECTS algorithms, but also perform slightly worse. The SHELF algorithms should only be favored if implementation simplicity is a concern" (§5).
- **What the survey leaves out.** It never frees a placed rectangle, and its benchmark allows rotating each rectangle by 90 degrees (§1). etagere's documentation says the paper "does not cover deallocation which complicates the problem space a fair bit" ([lib.rs#L14](https://github.com/nical/etagere/blob/2a178998597191e45631ea5f35d712352e15b0ef/src/lib.rs#L14)). None of the classes in RectangleBinPack has a remove method ([MaxRectsBinPack.h](https://github.com/juj/RectangleBinPack/blob/83e7e1132d93777e3732dfaae26b0f3703be2036/MaxRectsBinPack.h#L46)).

### Arriving one at a time, or a few per frame

- stb_rect_pack is Skyline Bottom-Left. It "does not do rotation", sorts the rectangles of each call by height, and warns that "calling this multiple times with multiple rect arrays will probably produce worse packing results than calling it a single time with the full rectangle array" ([stb_rect_pack.h#L5](https://github.com/nothings/stb/blob/9600522de53973336fa033a289858a4354cbb04c/stb_rect_pack.h#L5), [#L24](https://github.com/nothings/stb/blob/9600522de53973336fa033a289858a4354cbb04c/stb_rect_pack.h#L24), [#L106](https://github.com/nothings/stb/blob/9600522de53973336fa033a289858a4354cbb04c/stb_rect_pack.h#L106)).
- Dear ImGui packs each glyph with its own `stbrp_pack_rects` call of one rectangle, and its repack carries the note "Repacking in batch would be beneficial to packing heuristic, and fix stability" ([imgui_draw.cpp#L5955](https://github.com/ocornut/imgui/blob/77fe4588e6af934b4167b91312174ca3fbb0d71a/imgui_draw.cpp#L5955), [#L5636](https://github.com/ocornut/imgui/blob/77fe4588e6af934b4167b91312174ca3fbb0d71a/imgui_draw.cpp#L5638)).
- etagere's shelf allocator rounds an item's height up to a class (to a multiple of 8 below 32 texels, 16 below 128, 32 below 512, 64 above) so that items of near heights share shelves. It prefers the shortest shelf that fits, skipping shelves more than 1.5 times the item's height unless nothing else fits ([allocator.rs#L212](https://github.com/nical/etagere/blob/2a178998597191e45631ea5f35d712352e15b0ef/src/allocator.rs#L212), [#L816](https://github.com/nical/etagere/blob/2a178998597191e45631ea5f35d712352e15b0ef/src/allocator.rs#L816)). Its README says shelf packing "works very well when there is a high number of items with similar sizes, for example for dynamic glyph atlases".
- etagere's author reports, for WebRender, that the guillotine allocator "is the best at keeping track of all available space", "is the slowest when dealing with multiple thousands of items" and "can suffer from fragmentations in some of the workloads", while the shelf allocator "had a tendency to better deal with fragmentation" over long sessions. Moving images from WebRender's earlier slab allocator to etagere meant "what fit into five textures now fits into two or three" ([*Etagere*](https://nical.github.io/posts/etagere.html)).
- Godot's text server is a shelf packer that takes the first shelf of exactly the glyph's height, otherwise the shelf wasting the least area, otherwise a new shelf ([text_server_adv.h#L188](https://github.com/godotengine/godot/blob/ed1daf0bf001b61586d9930840f2f1394092c079/modules/text_server_adv/text_server_adv.h#L188)).
- TextMesh Pro's dynamic font assets add glyphs through Unity's TextCore with `GlyphPackingMode.BestShortSideFit`, keeping lists of free and used glyph rectangles across calls ([TMP_FontAsset.cs#L1513](https://github.com/needle-mirror/com.unity.textmeshpro/blob/dd55b58439c6204dd9ce0121084cf1e549f8d2cb/Scripts/Runtime/TMP_FontAsset.cs#L1513)). The modes are MaxRects' heuristics: best area fit, best long side fit, best short side fit, bottom-left ("in a Tetris like fashion") and contact point ([GlyphPackingMode](https://docs.unity.com/en-us/engine/current/script-reference/unityengine/textcore/lowlevel/glyphpackingmode)).

### v1's packer

v1's canvas packed into 2D-array textures of `AtlasSize` × `AtlasSize` × `LayersPerArray` (4096, 2 and a 256 MiB budget by default), RGBA8 sRGB, without mips ([config.go#L87](../../../../_legacy/bundles/canvas/internal/config.go#L87), [packer.go#L247](../../../../_legacy/bundles/canvas/internal/packer.go#L247)).

- **The algorithm is Shelf Next Fit per layer**, with first fit across layers and arrays. Each layer keeps one pen and one current row: an image that does not fit the row's width starts a new row below it, and rows left behind are never revisited ([packer.go#L107](../../../../_legacy/bundles/canvas/internal/packer.go#L107)). `place` first reuses a freed slot of exactly the same padded size, then tries every layer of every array in order, then makes a new array if the byte budget allows ([#L201](../../../../_legacy/bundles/canvas/internal/packer.go#L201)).
- **Freeing** pushes the slot onto a free list keyed by its exact size, and frees the whole array when its count of live slots reaches zero ([#L266](../../../../_legacy/bundles/canvas/internal/packer.go#L266)). A freed slot is reused only by an image of the same padded size, and a row's space is never merged.
- **Refusals** are two: an image whose padded rectangle is larger than a page (above 4092 texels for a sprite), and every layer full with the budget spent. Both are "terminal": the loader caches the zero entry ([#L83](../../../../_legacy/bundles/canvas/internal/packer.go#L83)). Sprites are padded by 2 texels, extruded or wrapped per axis, glyphs by 1 transparent texel ([spriteloader.go#L33](../../../../_legacy/bundles/canvas/internal/spriteloader.go#L33), [font.go#L156](../../../../_legacy/bundles/canvas/internal/font.go#L156)).
- **The lessons** in [render's v1 findings](../../../render/docs/research/v1-renderers.md) §4 are that glyph pages were freed only on a framebuffer scale change, and that a refusal made by the budget stayed cached after per-path unloads freed the space.

## 2. Pages: one big texture or layers of a 2D-array texture

### What WebGPU allows

| Limit | Default | Compatibility mode | What it bounds |
|---|---|---|---|
| `maxTextureDimension2D` | 8192 | 4096 | width and height of a `"2d"` texture |
| `maxTextureArrayLayers` | 256 | not read | `depthOrArrayLayers` of a `"2d"` texture |
| `maxSampledTexturesPerShaderStage` | 16 | not read | sampled textures across a pipeline layout |
| `maxBufferSize` | 256 MiB | not read | buffers, not textures |

From the spec's [limits](https://www.w3.org/TR/2026/CRD-webgpu-20260915/#limits) ([`maxTextureDimension2D`](https://www.w3.org/TR/2026/CRD-webgpu-20260915/#dom-supported-limits-maxtexturedimension2d), [`maxTextureArrayLayers`](https://www.w3.org/TR/2026/CRD-webgpu-20260915/#dom-supported-limits-maxtexturearraylayers)). gfx's `DefaultLimits` mirrors 8192 and 256 ([descriptors.go#L459](../../../gfx/descriptors.go#L459)). All are "maximum" limits: an adapter may offer more, never less.

- **Size is fixed at creation.** A texture's size, layer count, format and mip count are set by `createTexture`. A mip count may not exceed the [maximum mipLevel count](https://www.w3.org/TR/2026/CRD-webgpu-20260915/#abstract-opdef-maximum-miplevel-count), which for `"2d"` comes from width and height, not layers. Growing an array means a new texture and [`copyTextureToTexture`](https://www.w3.org/TR/2026/CRD-webgpu-20260915/#dom-gpucommandencoder-copytexturetotexture), which render offers as `CopyTexture` between passes ([render design](../../../render/docs/design.md#compute-copies-and-temporaries)).
- **A view's default dimension depends on the layer count.** When resolving a view's defaults, a `"2d"` texture whose "array layer count … is 1" gets a `"2d"` view, and otherwise `"2d-array"` ([resolving view defaults](https://www.w3.org/TR/2026/CRD-webgpu-20260915/#abstract-opdef-resolving-gputextureviewdescriptor-defaults)). A one-layer atlas bound as `texture_2d_array` needs an explicit view: render's `TextureView{…, Dimension}` ([render design](../../../render/docs/design.md#materials-and-parameters)). v1 required `LayersPerArray` of at least two; its sources read here do not say why.
- **Writing a page.** `queue.writeTexture` writes a region of one mip level of one layer ([writeTexture](https://www.w3.org/TR/2026/CRD-webgpu-20260915/#dom-gpuqueue-writetexture)). render's `inventory.WriteTexture(texture, TextureRegion{MipLevel, Layer, X, Y, Width, Height}, data)` mirrors it, rows packed tight ([render design](../../../render/docs/design.md#passes-command-lists-and-draws)). A compressed format's origin must be a multiple of its block size.
- **Memory, worked out (derived).** An RGBA8 page of 1024² is 4 MiB, 2048² is 16 MiB, 4096² is 64 MiB and 8192² is 256 MiB; a full mip chain adds a third. 256 layers of 2048² RGBA8 is 4 GiB. A single-channel glyph page is a quarter of these. Within the defaults, the bound a design meets first is memory.

### One texture against many

- **Binding.** All layers of an array sit behind one `texture_2d_array` binding, so sprites on different pages can share one material and one draw, the layer travelling as per-instance data (derived). Each separate texture is a binding of its own. TextMesh Pro's "Multi Atlas Textures" "enables the font asset to add new atlas textures as needed … when the main 1024 x 1024 is full" ([Unity forum, Stephan_B, 2022-11-21](https://discussions.unity.com/threads/unable-to-add-character-to-font-assets-atlas-texture.1363362/)).
- **WebRender keeps separate 2D textures and fights their number.** Each kind (RGBA8 linear, RGBA8 nearest, RGBA8 glyphs, A8, A8 glyphs, A16) is a list of `Texture2D` pages with its own budget, "separate to help with batching" ([texture_cache.rs#L254](https://github.com/servo/webrender/blob/e1c924ebad9ffdfe8c8c606aba77eb3f888c396a/webrender/src/texture_cache.rs#L254), [#L320](https://github.com/servo/webrender/blob/e1c924ebad9ffdfe8c8c606aba77eb3f888c396a/webrender/src/texture_cache.rs#L320)). "The entries that are needed in the current frame should be distributed across as few shared textures as possible, to minimize the number of draw calls. Ideally we only want one texture per type under simple workloads" ([#L1105](https://github.com/servo/webrender/blob/e1c924ebad9ffdfe8c8c606aba77eb3f888c396a/webrender/src/texture_cache.rs#L1105)).
- **Growing one texture.** Dear ImGui keeps one texture, starting at 512 × 128 and doubling towards a square up to 8192 × 8192, each growth a repack into a new texture ([imgui.h#L3923](https://github.com/ocornut/imgui/blob/77fe4588e6af934b4167b91312174ca3fbb0d71a/imgui.h#L3923), [imgui_draw.cpp#L5693](https://github.com/ocornut/imgui/blob/77fe4588e6af934b4167b91312174ca3fbb0d71a/imgui_draw.cpp#L5693)). glyphon starts at 256 and doubles up to the device's `max_texture_dimension_2d`, re-rasterising every cached glyph into the new texture ([text_atlas.rs#L33](https://github.com/grovesNL/glyphon/blob/49dc8f7bafa8091f4d71521fd62ee6f647b556f5/src/text_atlas.rs#L33), [#L112](https://github.com/grovesNL/glyphon/blob/49dc8f7bafa8091f4d71521fd62ee6f647b556f5/src/text_atlas.rs#L112)). fontstash leaves growth to its user: on `FONS_ATLAS_FULL`, `fonsExpandAtlas` copies the old pixels into a larger buffer ([fontstash.h#L1667](https://github.com/memononen/fontstash/blob/b5ddc9741061343740d85d636d782ed3e07cf7be/src/fontstash.h#L1667)).
- **Adding pages.** Bevy's font atlases, Godot's glyph textures and TextMesh Pro's multi-atlas option add a texture of a fixed size when none has room. v1 added a whole array of `LayersPerArray` layers.

| | Page | How it grows |
|---|---|---|
| WebRender | 2048² for RGBA8 linear and both glyph kinds, 512² for the others, two columns of shelves from 1024 up ([#L521](https://github.com/servo/webrender/blob/e1c924ebad9ffdfe8c8c606aba77eb3f888c396a/webrender/src/texture_cache.rs#L521)) | another texture of the kind |
| glyphon | one texture per kind (mask, colour), from 256² | doubles, re-rasterises |
| Dear ImGui 1.92 | one texture, 512 × 128 to 8192² | doubles, repacks |
| Bevy fonts | 512², or the next power of two holding a larger glyph | another texture |
| Godot glyphs | 256² to 1024² by font size (2048² for MSDF), larger for a larger glyph | another texture |
| TextMesh Pro | 1024² by default (per the forum post) | another texture with Multi Atlas Textures, else full |
| Godot `TextureAtlas` import | one texture, width up to `editor/import/atlas_max_width` (2048) | at import, width doubles while height exceeds twice the width |
| v1 canvas | 2D arrays of 4096² × 2 layers, 256 MiB budget | another array |

## 3. Freeing, repacking and eviction

### How a packer reclaims a freed region

- **Skyline: never one region.** stb_rect_pack has no free; to pack again "you need to call stbrp_init_target again" ([stb_rect_pack.h#L106](https://github.com/nothings/stb/blob/9600522de53973336fa033a289858a4354cbb04c/stb_rect_pack.h#L106)). fontstash offers `fonsResetAtlas`, which "resets the whole stash" ([fontstash.h#L106](https://github.com/memononen/fontstash/blob/b5ddc9741061343740d85d636d782ed3e07cf7be/src/fontstash.h#L106)).
- **Dear ImGui: mark, then repack.** Removing a rectangle only counts its area as discarded: "Existing pixels will stay in texture until resized / garbage collected" ([imgui.h#L3912](https://github.com/ocornut/imgui/blob/77fe4588e6af934b4167b91312174ca3fbb0d71a/imgui.h#L3912), [imgui_draw.cpp#L5916](https://github.com/ocornut/imgui/blob/77fe4588e6af934b4167b91312174ca3fbb0d71a/imgui_draw.cpp#L5916)). The space returns when everything live is packed again into a new texture, its pixels copied and its glyphs' UVs patched ([#L5622](https://github.com/ocornut/imgui/blob/77fe4588e6af934b4167b91312174ca3fbb0d71a/imgui_draw.cpp#L5622)).
- **Shelf (etagere `AtlasAllocator`): per item.** Deallocation merges the freed item with free neighbours on its shelf; an empty shelf merges with empty neighbouring shelves in its column ([allocator.rs#L394](https://github.com/nical/etagere/blob/2a178998597191e45631ea5f35d712352e15b0ef/src/allocator.rs#L394)). It "does a reasonable job of dealing with fragmentation, at a runtime cost" ([lib.rs#L24](https://github.com/nical/etagere/blob/2a178998597191e45631ea5f35d712352e15b0ef/src/lib.rs#L24)).
- **Shelf (etagere `BucketedAtlasAllocator`): per bucket.** "Space is only reclaimed when all items of the same bucket are deallocated", and only the top-most shelf is removed when empty ([bucketed.rs#L60](https://github.com/nical/etagere/blob/2a178998597191e45631ea5f35d712352e15b0ef/src/bucketed.rs#L60)). Its author found it fine for glyphs and poor for large images, where held space costs more ([*Etagere*](https://nical.github.io/posts/etagere.html)).
- **Guillotine (guillotiere): per item, in constant time.** A tree of containers whose children tile their parent lets two free siblings merge at once, instead of "examining all possible pairs of free rectangles". It misses merges across two containers split the same way, and is "not the best solution for very 'structured' grid-like subdivision patterns" ([allocator.rs#L141](https://github.com/nical/guillotiere/blob/9c8933a7d7eea41097f1468367f33131cd96b083/src/allocator.rs#L141), [#L279](https://github.com/nical/guillotiere/blob/9c8933a7d7eea41097f1468367f33131cd96b083/src/allocator.rs#L279)). Bevy's `DynamicTextureAtlasBuilder` wraps it but exposes no removal ([dynamic_texture_atlas_builder.rs#L29](https://github.com/bevyengine/bevy/blob/a0577b5c6a7b208719dd761c5c6a7a539e472a97/crates/bevy_image/src/dynamic_texture_atlas_builder.rs#L29)).
- **MaxRects: none read.** TextMesh Pro removes no single glyph: `ClearFontAssetData` "clears font asset data including the glyph and character tables and textures" ([TMP_FontAsset API](https://docs.unity3d.com/Packages/com.unity.ugui@2.0/api/TMPro.TMP_FontAsset.html)).

### When engines repack or defragment

- **guillotiere's `rearrange`** packs every live allocation again, largest first, into a cleared allocator, and returns a change list of old and new rectangles and failures: "Previous ids and rectangles are not valid anymore" ([allocator.rs#L720](https://github.com/nical/guillotiere/blob/9c8933a7d7eea41097f1468367f33131cd96b083/src/allocator.rs#L721)). Moving the pixels is the caller's.
- **Dear ImGui** repacks when a glyph does not fit: it first discards font sizes unused for 2 frames, then repacks at the same size if the discarded area is at least 20% of the packed area, and grows otherwise ([imgui_draw.cpp#L5725](https://github.com/ocornut/imgui/blob/77fe4588e6af934b4167b91312174ca3fbb0d71a/imgui_draw.cpp#L5725)). It makes space this way up to three times, packing four times in all, before returning a fallback glyph ([#L5955](https://github.com/ocornut/imgui/blob/77fe4588e6af934b4167b91312174ca3fbb0d71a/imgui_draw.cpp#L5955)).
- **WebRender compacts a little each frame.** It picks one kind of texture, moves allocations from the last texture into the first until one fails to fit, at most 512 × 512 moved texels per frame "to avoid causing frame spikes on low end GPUs", and turns to another kind when there is nothing to move ([texture_cache.rs#L759](https://github.com/servo/webrender/blob/e1c924ebad9ffdfe8c8c606aba77eb3f888c396a/webrender/src/texture_cache.rs#L759), [texture_pack/mod.rs#L315](https://github.com/servo/webrender/blob/e1c924ebad9ffdfe8c8c606aba77eb3f888c396a/webrender/src/texture_pack/mod.rs#L315)). Empty textures are released at the end of the frame, so a texture emptied by eviction at its start can be reused within it ([#L708](https://github.com/servo/webrender/blob/e1c924ebad9ffdfe8c8c606aba77eb3f888c396a/webrender/src/texture_cache.rs#L716)).
- **glyphon** never repacks in place; growing re-rasterises every cached glyph into the new texture.

### How engines evict

| | What is evicted | When | Policy |
|---|---|---|---|
| WebRender | entries with automatic eviction; manual ones count against the budget but stay | start of each frame | least recently used per budget; never an entry used in the previous frame; an entry must be unused for 400 frames below the threshold (evicting there only while the kind has several textures), then 200, 100, 50, 25, 10 and 5 frames at 1× to 6× the threshold, and 1 frame from 7×; at most 32 evictions a frame unless over 4× |
| glyphon | single glyphs | when an allocation fails | least recently used; a glyph used since the last `trim`, which a frame calls, is never evicted; then grow |
| Dear ImGui | whole baked font sizes | when a glyph does not fit | sizes unused for 2 frames, unless the font locks its sizes |
| Bevy fonts | nothing | — | `FontAtlasSet::clear` by hand |
| Godot glyphs | nothing on its own | — | `font_remove_glyph` drops the map entry only; `font_clear_textures`, `clear_cache` by hand |
| TextMesh Pro | nothing on its own | — | `ClearFontAssetData`; "Clear Dynamic Data on Build" |
| fontstash | — | `FONS_ATLAS_FULL` | the user's callback may expand or reset |
| v1 | sprites by path; glyph pages never | on unload | count-free: an array goes when its last slot is freed |

Sources: WebRender [texture_cache.rs#L1096–L1246](https://github.com/servo/webrender/blob/e1c924ebad9ffdfe8c8c606aba77eb3f888c396a/webrender/src/texture_cache.rs#L1154); glyphon [text_atlas.rs#L73](https://github.com/grovesNL/glyphon/blob/49dc8f7bafa8091f4d71521fd62ee6f647b556f5/src/text_atlas.rs#L73), [#L220](https://github.com/grovesNL/glyphon/blob/49dc8f7bafa8091f4d71521fd62ee6f647b556f5/src/text_atlas.rs#L220); Dear ImGui [imgui_draw.cpp#L5489](https://github.com/ocornut/imgui/blob/77fe4588e6af934b4167b91312174ca3fbb0d71a/imgui_draw.cpp#L5489); Bevy [font_atlas_set.rs#L52](https://github.com/bevyengine/bevy/blob/a0577b5c6a7b208719dd761c5c6a7a539e472a97/crates/bevy_text/src/font_atlas_set.rs#L52); Godot [text_server_adv.cpp#L3496](https://github.com/godotengine/godot/blob/ed1daf0bf001b61586d9930840f2f1394092c079/modules/text_server_adv/text_server_adv.cpp#L3496), [TextServer.xml](https://github.com/godotengine/godot/blob/ed1daf0bf001b61586d9930840f2f1394092c079/doc/classes/TextServer.xml#L86); TextMesh Pro, the forum post above.

- **WebRender's thresholds.** For glyph kinds the threshold is two thirds of one texture's bytes, because "glyphs are usually small and tightly packed so they waste very little space"; for others, one third, since mixed sizes are "more prone to wasting pixels". Above it, eviction continues while more than one texture of the kind is in use, because "the only way to get rid of that fragmentation is to continue evicting". The aim: "even after a heavy workload, the shared cache settles back to a single texture atlas per type over some reasonable period of time" ([texture_cache.rs#L1105–L1146](https://github.com/servo/webrender/blob/e1c924ebad9ffdfe8c8c606aba77eb3f888c396a/webrender/src/texture_cache.rs#L1113)). Standalone textures are evicted only above 8 MiB, since "unused textures don't cause extra draw calls".
- **Against v1's two lessons.** Engines that bound their glyph memory evict by last use: WebRender by frame age, glyphon by the frame, Dear ImGui by font size. None ties eviction to a count of users. Each treats "full" as a moment to evict, compact, repack or grow and then retry, and fails an insert only when nothing evictable is left (glyphon: every glyph is in use this frame; Dear ImGui: after three rounds of making space, or with resizing locked).

## 4. Edges: padding, extrusion, bleeding, mips and rotation

### Bleeding under linear filtering

- **Why.** Sampling a texel at its centre takes that texel alone; off centre, bilinear filtering mixes its neighbours, "behavior to be avoided, however, for texels lining the border of sub-textures in an atlas, as they are in danger of pulling in texels from foreign textures". UVs from 0 to 1 inclusive reach half a texel past the image on each side, so the NVIDIA tool offers a `-halftexel` option to rescale them inwards ([NVIDIA](https://download.nvidia.com/developer/NVTextureSuite/Atlas_Tools/Texture_Atlas_Whitepaper.pdf), pp. 11–12).
- **Padding.** Engines leave a gutter. Dear ImGui: "Defaults to 1. If your rendering method never relies on bilinear filtering you may set this to 0" ([imgui.h#L3922](https://github.com/ocornut/imgui/blob/77fe4588e6af934b4167b91312174ca3fbb0d71a/imgui.h#L3922)). Unity's sprite atlas: "Sets how many pixels Unity uses between individual sprites, to prevent pixels from one sprite appearing in an adjacent sprite. The default value is 4" ([Sprite Atlas reference](https://docs.unity.com/en-us/engine/6000.0/manual/unity2d/sprite/atlas/sprite-atlas-reference)). Bevy's glyph atlases pad by 2, its `TextureAtlasBuilder` by 0 by default, on the right and bottom only ([font_atlas.rs#L11](https://github.com/bevyengine/bevy/blob/a0577b5c6a7b208719dd761c5c6a7a539e472a97/crates/bevy_text/src/font_atlas.rs#L11), [texture_atlas_builder.rs#L54](https://github.com/bevyengine/bevy/blob/a0577b5c6a7b208719dd761c5c6a7a539e472a97/crates/bevy_image/src/texture_atlas_builder.rs#L54)). fontstash pads by the blur radius plus 2 ([fontstash.h#L1092](https://github.com/memononen/fontstash/blob/b5ddc9741061343740d85d636d782ed3e07cf7be/src/fontstash.h#L1092)). Godot's glyphs take 2 texels a side ([text_server_adv.h#L145](https://github.com/godotengine/godot/blob/ed1daf0bf001b61586d9930840f2f1394092c079/modules/text_server_adv/text_server_adv.h#L145), [text_server_adv.cpp#L1189](https://github.com/godotengine/godot/blob/ed1daf0bf001b61586d9930840f2f1394092c079/modules/text_server_adv/text_server_adv.cpp#L1189)). Godot's atlas import grows each image's cell bitmap by one 4-texel cell a side ([editor_atlas_packer.cpp#L92](https://github.com/godotengine/godot/blob/ed1daf0bf001b61586d9930840f2f1394092c079/editor/import/editor_atlas_packer.cpp#L92)).
- **What fills the gutter.** A transparent gutter is right for glyphs and for sprites whose edges are already transparent. v1 extruded a sprite's edge texels outwards, or wrapped the opposite edge in on an axis that tiles, because "an edge that repeats wants the edge it wraps around to, an edge that ends wants its own texels continued" ([packer.go#L57](../../../../_legacy/bundles/canvas/internal/packer.go#L57)). Unity's "Alpha Dilation" "expands the colors of the edge of the sprite into adjacent transparent pixels, to prevent visible edges". Godot fills new glyph textures with white at zero alpha "to prevent artifacts when the font is displayed at a non-default scale with filtering enabled" ([text_server_adv.cpp#L866](https://github.com/godotengine/godot/blob/ed1daf0bf001b61586d9930840f2f1394092c079/modules/text_server_adv/text_server_adv.cpp#L871)); v1's glyphs are white with coverage in alpha for the same reason.
- **Alignment.** WebRender rounds allocations up to 8 × 8 texels (16 × 16 for RGBA8 linear images, 4 × 8 for glyphs) ([texture_cache.rs#L320](https://github.com/servo/webrender/blob/e1c924ebad9ffdfe8c8c606aba77eb3f888c396a/webrender/src/texture_cache.rs#L320)). The source read does not say why.

### Mips

- **Copy each image's chain, generated alone.** NVIDIA: "the tool-chain generates the mip-maps for individual textures before these are packed into an atlas … Because texels are never combined--just copied--no smearing or cross-pollution occurs." Its tool copies each image's chain into the atlas's levels (pp. 4, 8).
- **A box filter over the whole atlas** is clean only "if the atlas is a power-of-two texture and contains only power-of-two textures that do not unnecessarily cross power-of-two lines"; even then the levels below a sub-texture's own 1 × 1 mix sub-textures (pp. 4–6). Those deepest levels are reached only by triangles smaller than half a pixel, so NVIDIA calls them harmless and suggests not storing them: "a 1kx1k atlas containing 16 256x256 textures should only store 8 mip-levels" (pp. 7–8).
- **Bilinear sampling of lower levels still bleeds.** Coordinates at a texel's centre in level 0 are off centre in level 1, so "bilinear and anisotropic filtering of all lower mip-maps also access unrelated neighboring texels". NVIDIA calls these artifacts "not easily overcome" though small (p. 12).
- **Gutter per level (derived).** One texel of level k covers 2^k texels of level 0. Keeping level k's bilinear footprint inside an image's own texels and gutter needs a gutter of 2^k texels at level 0, and an origin aligned to 2^k so the image's rectangle is whole at level k. Four clean levels below the first need an 8-texel gutter and 8-texel alignment.
- **What render does.** render generates mips only for textures loaded with `Mipmaps`. Each level below the first, of each layer, is a pass that draws one triangle over it, "sampling the level above, of the same layer, through a linear filter" ([render design](../../../render/docs/design.md#the-frame-on-the-kernel)). Over an atlas page, that is the whole-atlas box filter above. `WriteTexture` names a mip level, so a packer can write each image's own levels instead.
- **What engines do.** Unity's sprite atlas has "Generate Mip Maps" beside its padding. Godot's glyph textures generate mips over the whole texture when the font asks for them, with the 2-texel margin ([ResourceImporterDynamicFont.xml#L37](https://github.com/godotengine/godot/blob/ed1daf0bf001b61586d9930840f2f1394092c079/doc/classes/ResourceImporterDynamicFont.xml#L37), [text_server_adv.cpp#L3398](https://github.com/godotengine/godot/blob/ed1daf0bf001b61586d9930840f2f1394092c079/modules/text_server_adv/text_server_adv.cpp#L3398)). v1, WebRender's shared cache, glyphon and Dear ImGui read here make no mips for their atlases.

### Rotation

- **Who rotates.** Jylänki's survey assumes rotation by 90 degrees is allowed, and `MaxRectsBinPack` takes `allowFlip`, true by default ([MaxRectsBinPack.h#L25](https://github.com/juj/RectangleBinPack/blob/83e7e1132d93777e3732dfaae26b0f3703be2036/MaxRectsBinPack.h#L25)). stb_rect_pack, etagere and guillotiere do not rotate. Unity's "Allow Rotation" "rotates sprites in the sprite atlas texture to find a better fit. Disable this property if you pack Canvas UI elements, to avoid UI elements rotating."
- **Godot rotates only meshes.** Its atlas import transposes an image taller than wide only in Mesh2D mode, where the mesh carries the UVs; in Region mode, which draws a rectangle, `can_transpose` is false ([resource_importer_texture_atlas.cpp#L244](https://github.com/godotengine/godot/blob/ed1daf0bf001b61586d9930840f2f1394092c079/editor/import/resource_importer_texture_atlas.cpp#L244), [#L260](https://github.com/godotengine/godot/blob/ed1daf0bf001b61586d9930840f2f1394092c079/editor/import/resource_importer_texture_atlas.cpp#L260)).
- **The cost (derived).** A rotated image swaps its UV axes, so every vertex, and every material that reads UV direction (a scroll along u, a gradient, a 9-slice border), must know it was rotated.

## 5. Left out: oversized, tiled and repeating images

- **Oversized.** WebRender's shared cache takes images up to 512 texels high (`TEXTURE_REGION_DIMENSIONS`), and up to 512 wide, or up to half its texture's side for RGBA8 linear images (1024 at the default 2048); larger images, and nearest-filtered alpha images, get a standalone texture ([texture_cache.rs#L1358](https://github.com/servo/webrender/blob/e1c924ebad9ffdfe8c8c606aba77eb3f888c396a/webrender/src/texture_cache.rs#L1358)). Unity: "If any of the sprites or textures are larger than the Max Texture Size, Unity ignores the Max Texture Size setting and uses the minimum size that contains the sprites". Bevy's fonts and Godot's glyphs make a page large enough for the glyph; Godot refuses a glyph whose padded side exceeds 4096 texels ([font_atlas.rs#L160](https://github.com/bevyengine/bevy/blob/a0577b5c6a7b208719dd761c5c6a7a539e472a97/crates/bevy_text/src/font_atlas.rs#L160), [text_server_adv.cpp#L850](https://github.com/godotengine/godot/blob/ed1daf0bf001b61586d9930840f2f1394092c079/modules/text_server_adv/text_server_adv.cpp#L850), [#L1189](https://github.com/godotengine/godot/blob/ed1daf0bf001b61586d9930840f2f1394092c079/modules/text_server_adv/text_server_adv.cpp#L1189)). Dear ImGui grows its texture to the next power of two above the largest rectangle. Bevy's `TextureAtlasBuilder` returns `NotEnoughSpace` and recommends "a larger sprite sheet" ([texture_atlas_builder.rs#L198](https://github.com/bevyengine/bevy/blob/a0577b5c6a7b208719dd761c5c6a7a539e472a97/crates/bevy_image/src/texture_atlas_builder.rs#L198)). v1 refused a sprite above 4092 texels for good.
- **Repeating with the sampler does not work.** Inside an atlas "a GPU's address modes never apply", and coordinates outside 0 to 1 reach neighbouring images. Replicating the image is wasteful; instead "pixel-shaders are able to emulate these address modes", which needs `ddx` and `ddy` (NVIDIA, pp. 9–10). Wrapping with `fract` breaks the derivatives a mip lookup takes at each seam (derived), which is why the emulation needs them.
- **Tiling with geometry.** Bevy's `SpriteImageMode::Tiled { tile_x, tile_y, stretch_value }` is drawn as slices, quads that each cover one repeat, so an atlas region tiles with no repeat sampler ([sprite.rs#L202](https://github.com/bevyengine/bevy/blob/a0577b5c6a7b208719dd761c5c6a7a539e472a97/crates/bevy_sprite/src/sprite.rs#L202)). Unity's Tiled draw mode "repeats" a sprite's sections as its size changes ([9-slicing, 2020.1](https://docs.unity3d.com/2020.1/Documentation/Manual/9SliceSprites.html)).
- **v1** wrapped in the shader, with a wrapped gutter on the tiled axes, and gave oversized tiled sprites "a full-image texture of its own, kept outside the atlas so tiled sprites can sample it with repeat addressing" ([spriteloader.go#L130](../../../../_legacy/bundles/canvas/internal/spriteloader.go#L140)).
- **Kinds kept apart.** WebRender gives each format and filter its own atlas, and glyphs their own "to help with batching". Unity advises separate atlases for sprites used together, for different compression, and for frequently and infrequently used sprites ([Sprite atlases](https://docs.unity.com/en-us/engine/6000.0/manual/unity2d/sprite/atlas/atlas-introduction)). Godot: "Texture atlases are only supported in 2D rendering, not 3D" ([ResourceImporterTextureAtlas.xml#L7](https://github.com/godotengine/godot/blob/ed1daf0bf001b61586d9930840f2f1394092c079/doc/classes/ResourceImporterTextureAtlas.xml#L7)).

## 6. Engines: what is packed, and when

| | What is packed | Decided by | When | Packer |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| Unity Sprite Atlas | the sprites, textures and folders in its "Objects for Packing" | the user's atlas asset | in the editor, or on entering Play mode and at build ("Enabled for Builds") | not documented; "Tight Packing" uses each sprite's outline |
| TextMesh Pro dynamic font | each glyph text uses | first use | run time | MaxRects, best short side fit |
| Godot `TextureAtlas` | images marked "Import As TextureAtlas" into one atlas file | the user's import settings | editor import | skyline over 4-texel cells of each image's trimmed rectangle or alpha polygons |
| Godot text server | each glyph, per font size | first use, or a `preload` range at import | run time | shelf |
| Bevy `TextureAtlasBuilder` | the images added to it | the user's code | run time, a known set at once | the rectangle-pack crate |
| Bevy `DynamicTextureAtlasBuilder` | each image added | the user's code | run time, one at a time | guillotiere |
| Bevy fonts | each glyph, per font, size and rendering key | first use | run time | guillotiere through the dynamic builder |
| Dear ImGui 1.92 | each glyph, per baked size | first use | run time | stb skyline |
| fontstash | each glyph, per size and blur | first use | run time | skyline after Jylänki |
| WebRender | images and glyphs that fit the shared cache | size, format, filter | run time | etagere shelves |
| v1 canvas | every sprite and glyph drawn, except oversized tiled sprites | first draw | run time | shelf next fit per layer |

### Unity

- **Sprite atlases are built, not grown.** With "Sprite Atlas V2 - Enabled for Builds", "Unity now builds and uses sprite atlases only when you enter Play mode or build your project". Otherwise "Pack Preview" packs in the editor, and sprites "render using their sprite atlas texture in the Scene view, the Game view, and in Play mode" ([Create a sprite atlas](https://docs.unity.com/en-us/engine/6000.0/manual/unity2d/sprite/atlas/create-sprite-atlas)). "If you add a sprite to multiple sprite atlases, Unity randomly chooses which sprite atlas texture to use at runtime."
- **At run time Unity loads atlases, it does not pack them.** With "Include in Build" off, "your sprites still reference the sprite atlas … but the sprite atlas texture is missing", and `SpriteAtlasManager.atlasRequested` "triggers when a sprite requests an unloaded sprite atlas", for the game to load it: late binding ([Load sprite atlases manually](https://docs.unity.com/en-us/engine/6000.0/manual/unity2d/sprite/atlas/load-sprite-atlas-spriteatlasmanageratlasrequested)).
- **Why atlas.** "Unity has to create and send a separate draw call to the GPU for each texture … Unity only needs to create one draw call for all the sprites in a sprite atlas" ([Sprite atlases](https://docs.unity.com/en-us/engine/6000.0/manual/unity2d/sprite/atlas/atlas-introduction)).
- **TextMesh Pro.** A dynamic font asset starts "with an empty atlas to which characters are added automatically as you use them", and its source font ships in the build ([Dynamic fonts assets, 3.2](https://docs.unity3d.com/Packages/com.unity.textmeshpro@3.2/manual/FontAssetsDynamicFonts.html)). "Clear Dynamic Data on Build" "clears the Character and Glyph tables … when creating a build or closing the Editor" and resets the atlas to size zero (forum post above).

### Godot

- **Sprite atlases are an import.** `ResourceImporterTextureAtlas` "imports a collection of textures from a PNG image into an [AtlasTexture] or 2D [ArrayMesh]", in Region mode a rectangle (trimmed of transparent borders by default) and in Mesh2D mode a polygon that cuts fill rate ([ResourceImporterTextureAtlas.xml](https://github.com/godotengine/godot/blob/ed1daf0bf001b61586d9930840f2f1394092c079/doc/classes/ResourceImporterTextureAtlas.xml#L20)). `chart_pack` sorts the images by area, largest first, drops each column profile onto a height map at its lowest position, and doubles the width while the height exceeds twice it, up to `editor/import/atlas_max_width`, 2048 by default ([editor_atlas_packer.cpp#L175](https://github.com/godotengine/godot/blob/ed1daf0bf001b61586d9930840f2f1394092c079/editor/import/editor_atlas_packer.cpp#L175), [editor_atlas_packer.h#L62](https://github.com/godotengine/godot/blob/ed1daf0bf001b61586d9930840f2f1394092c079/editor/import/editor_atlas_packer.h#L62)). No run-time sprite atlas was found in the sources read.
- **Glyphs are packed at run time**, into a list of textures per font size ([text_server_adv.cpp#L823](https://github.com/godotengine/godot/blob/ed1daf0bf001b61586d9930840f2f1394092c079/modules/text_server_adv/text_server_adv.cpp#L823)). The importer's `preload` renders glyph ranges ahead "to avoid stuttering during gameplay when new characters need to be rendered", at the cost of load time and memory ([ResourceImporterDynamicFont.xml#L74](https://github.com/godotengine/godot/blob/ed1daf0bf001b61586d9930840f2f1394092c079/doc/classes/ResourceImporterDynamicFont.xml#L74)).

### Bevy

- **`TextureAtlasBuilder`** packs the images added to it when `build` is called, starting at 256² and doubling both sides up to `max_size`, 2048² by default, until the set fits, and returns one image with a `TextureAtlasLayout` of rectangles ([texture_atlas_builder.rs#L203](https://github.com/bevyengine/bevy/blob/a0577b5c6a7b208719dd761c5c6a7a539e472a97/crates/bevy_image/src/texture_atlas_builder.rs#L203)). A `Sprite` names an image and an optional `TextureAtlas` layout and index, so the user decides what is atlased ([sprite.rs#L17](https://github.com/bevyengine/bevy/blob/a0577b5c6a7b208719dd761c5c6a7a539e472a97/crates/bevy_sprite/src/sprite.rs#L17)).
- **`DynamicTextureAtlasBuilder`** adds one image at a time into a fixed-size atlas through guillotiere, "helpful in cases when texture is created procedurally, e.g: in a font glyph" atlas, and fails with `FailedToAllocateSpace` when full ([dynamic_texture_atlas_builder.rs#L64](https://github.com/bevyengine/bevy/blob/a0577b5c6a7b208719dd761c5c6a7a539e472a97/crates/bevy_image/src/dynamic_texture_atlas_builder.rs#L27)).
- **Fonts.** A `FontAtlasSet` maps each font, size and rendering key to a list of `FontAtlas`. A glyph goes into the first atlas with room, or into a new one of at least 512² ([font_atlas.rs#L138](https://github.com/bevyengine/bevy/blob/a0577b5c6a7b208719dd761c5c6a7a539e472a97/crates/bevy_text/src/font_atlas.rs#L138), [font_atlas_set.rs#L29](https://github.com/bevyengine/bevy/blob/a0577b5c6a7b208719dd761c5c6a7a539e472a97/crates/bevy_text/src/font_atlas_set.rs#L29)).

### Glyph caches: Dear ImGui and fontstash

- **Dear ImGui 1.92** made fonts sizeable at any size: "Before 1.92, fonts were of a single size. They can now be dynamically sized", and "specifying glyph ranges is now unnecessary" ([CHANGELOG](https://github.com/ocornut/imgui/blob/77fe4588e6af934b4167b91312174ca3fbb0d71a/docs/CHANGELOG.txt#L1949)). Glyphs are baked per font and size on first use into one texture; the policy is in sections 2 and 3.
- **fontstash** packs glyphs with "Skyline Bin Packer by Jukka Jylänki" into one texture. When full, "let the user to resize the atlas (or not), and try again" ([fontstash.h#L516](https://github.com/memononen/fontstash/blob/b5ddc9741061343740d85d636d782ed3e07cf7be/src/fontstash.h#L516), [#L1130](https://github.com/memononen/fontstash/blob/b5ddc9741061343740d85d636d782ed3e07cf7be/src/fontstash.h#L1130)).

## Not verified

- **Unity's sprite packer.** The algorithm behind Unity's sprite atlases, the default "Max Texture Size", and whether an atlas that overflows it becomes several pages are not in the pages read.
- **TextMesh Pro, current.** Its packing was read in `TMP_FontAsset.cs` at package 3.0.9; the class is not in later versions of the mirror, so the packing mode in Unity 6's TextMesh Pro was not read. That each extra atlas texture costs a draw call was found only in a search summary, not in the forum post it was attributed to. The 1024² default comes from that forum post, not the manual.
- **Unity's Tiled draw mode** is described as repeating sections; whether it does so with geometry, as Bevy does, was not read.
- **Compatibility-mode limits.** The spec's compatibility default for `maxTextureArrayLayers` was not read; only the 4096 for `maxTextureDimension2D` was.
- **Jylänki's scores** were decoded from the PDF's custom font encoding. They agree with the paper's prose for every value the prose quotes, but the row labels of tables 7 and 15 are offset in the extracted text, so per-heuristic values within a table may be swapped; the ranges given above hold.
- **The scores' workload.** They come from synthetic rectangles of mixed sizes, not from sprites or glyphs, and no source read measured fill rates with freeing. etagere's author gives only the "five textures … two or three" comparison.
- **Rotation, gutter per mip level, and fill with `fract`** are reasoning in this file, not claims of the sources.
- **Why v1 needed two layers,** and why WebRender aligns allocations to 4–16 texels, were not found in the sources read.
- **Godot's run-time 2D.** That Godot has no run-time sprite atlas rests on not finding one in the folders read (`editor/import`, the text servers, `scene/resources`), not on a statement.

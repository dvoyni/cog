# What the gogpu fork lacks for platform's window and input seams

Research for [cog#718](https://github.com/dvoyni/cog/issues/718), on the map [cog#611](https://github.com/dvoyni/cog/issues/611). Gathered 2026-11-07.

The gogpu plugin implements platform's `WindowsSocket` and `InputSourceSocket` [platform/sockets.go:1-50](https://github.com/dvoyni/cog/blob/6581d1b0bab1c6b365fee243f0b3eb7c0554bd92/platform/sockets.go#L1-L50) over the fork's `HostLoop` and its `App` and `Window` API. [cog#712](https://github.com/dvoyni/cog/issues/712) and [cog#715](https://github.com/dvoyni/cog/issues/715) already decided that the fork gains a `HostLoop.Start` with no window, one method that opens any window with its surface, and resizes applied at `BeginFrame` for every window. The GPU side of starting and opening windows is in [What gogpu's HostLoop needs to start](https://github.com/dvoyni/cog/blob/research/gogpu-device-and-start/research/gogpu-device-and-start.md) ([cog#713](https://github.com/dvoyni/cog/issues/713)) and is not repeated here.

This note measures every behaviour of the two sockets, per OS, against the fork at its pin, and answers each with **present**, **present with a small fork change** (named Fn, listed at the end), or **missing**.

## How the plugin reaches the fork

- **Only the `App` and `Window` API is public.** The per-OS layers sit behind `internal/platform`'s `PlatformManager` and `PlatformWindow` [platform.go:143-347](https://github.com/dvoyni/gogpu/blob/92d976d655fb366878fa120272e97093bf71cf1b/internal/platform/platform.go#L143-L347), which the plugin cannot import. Everything the plugin needs must be on `App`, `Window` or `HostLoop`.
- **`HostLoop.PumpEvents` is `App.processEventsMultiThread`** [hostloop.go:72-74](https://github.com/dvoyni/gogpu/blob/92d976d655fb366878fa120272e97093bf71cf1b/hostloop.go#L72-L74): it drains `PollEvents` and calls `classifyEvent` for each event in order [app.go:719-778](https://github.com/dvoyni/gogpu/blob/92d976d655fb366878fa120272e97093bf71cf1b/app.go#L719-L778) [app.go:803-869](https://github.com/dvoyni/gogpu/blob/92d976d655fb366878fa120272e97093bf71cf1b/app.go#L803-L869).
- **Three input outputs exist, and only one names the window.**
  - The input-event queue (`App.PollInputEvent`, [app.go:1584-1591](https://github.com/dvoyni/gogpu/blob/92d976d655fb366878fa120272e97093bf71cf1b/app.go#L1584-L1591)) carries gpucontext events with no window: `KeyEvent`, `CharEvent`, `FocusEvent`, `ResizeEvent` have no window field [gpucontext/input_event.go:50-108](https://github.com/gogpu/gpucontext/blob/v0.31.3/input_event.go#L50-L108).
  - `EventSource` callbacks have no window either [event_source.go:45-145](https://github.com/dvoyni/gogpu/blob/92d976d655fb366878fa120272e97093bf71cf1b/event_source.go#L45-L145).
  - **Per-window callbacks on `*Window` do:** `SetOnClose`, `SetOnKeyPress`, `SetOnKeyRelease`, `SetOnTextInput`, `SetOnPointer`, `SetOnScroll`, `SetOnResize` [window_manager.go:59-124](https://github.com/dvoyni/gogpu/blob/92d976d655fb366878fa120272e97093bf71cf1b/window_manager.go#L59-L124). `classifyEvent` routes key, char, pointer and scroll events to them by the event's platform window ID [app.go:872-947](https://github.com/dvoyni/gogpu/blob/92d976d655fb366878fa120272e97093bf71cf1b/app.go#L872-L947). **The plugin builds every `InputChange` from these per-window callbacks**, which keep the platform's event order.
- **`Window` has no focus or scale callback,** and focus reaches only app-wide outputs (below, F4).
- **Key repeat is not flagged.** gpucontext's `KeyEvent` has no `Repeat` [gpucontext/input_event.go:50-54](https://github.com/gogpu/gpucontext/blob/v0.31.3/input_event.go#L50-L54), and the per-window callback takes a key and modifiers only. Every OS layer reports a held key's repeats as further key-downs with no key-up between (see the table), so the plugin marks a press of a key it already holds as a repeat. No fork change.
- **Mapping onto cog's types.** gpucontext's `Key` names every key cog's `Key` names, by physical position [gpucontext/events.go:153-343](https://github.com/gogpu/gpucontext/blob/v0.31.3/events.go#L153-L343), but numbers them with gaps, while cog's are dense [platform/key.go:18-170](https://github.com/dvoyni/cog/blob/6581d1b0bab1c6b365fee243f0b3eb7c0554bd92/platform/key.go#L18-L170): the plugin maps by table, not by the cast the legacy extension used [_legacy/extensions/gogpu/internal/input.go:110-114](https://github.com/dvoyni/cog/blob/6581d1b0bab1c6b365fee243f0b3eb7c0554bd92/_legacy/extensions/gogpu/internal/input.go#L110-L114). `Modifiers` share bit positions, so a cast works [gpucontext/events.go:678-697](https://github.com/gogpu/gpucontext/blob/v0.31.3/events.go#L678-L697) [platform/modifiers.go:1-22](https://github.com/dvoyni/cog/blob/6581d1b0bab1c6b365fee243f0b3eb7c0554bd92/platform/modifiers.go#L1-L22) [_legacy/extensions/gogpu/internal/input.go:122-126](https://github.com/dvoyni/cog/blob/6581d1b0bab1c6b365fee243f0b3eb7c0554bd92/_legacy/extensions/gogpu/internal/input.go#L122-L126). Mouse buttons map as the legacy `mapPointerButton` does [_legacy/extensions/gogpu/internal/input.go:76-95](https://github.com/dvoyni/cog/blob/6581d1b0bab1c6b365fee243f0b3eb7c0554bd92/_legacy/extensions/gogpu/internal/input.go#L76-L95). Cursor shapes are the same eleven in the same order, and gpucontext's twelfth, `CursorNone`, hides [gpucontext/platform.go:157-195](https://github.com/gogpu/gpucontext/blob/v0.31.3/platform.go#L157-L195) [platform/cursorrequest.go:17-36](https://github.com/dvoyni/cog/blob/6581d1b0bab1c6b365fee243f0b3eb7c0554bd92/platform/cursorrequest.go#L17-L36).

## Windows at runtime

### Start and open

- `HostLoop.Start` creates the primary window, its surface and the device together and shows the window [hostloop.go:185-207](https://github.com/dvoyni/gogpu/blob/92d976d655fb366878fa120272e97093bf71cf1b/hostloop.go#L185-L207); `App.NewWindow` refuses to run before it [window_manager.go:334-337](https://github.com/dvoyni/gogpu/blob/92d976d655fb366878fa120272e97093bf71cf1b/window_manager.go#L334-L337) and shows every window it opens [window_manager.go:395-397](https://github.com/dvoyni/gogpu/blob/92d976d655fb366878fa120272e97093bf71cf1b/window_manager.go#L395-L397). F1, already decided, replaces both with a window-less start and one open method. Its detail is in the cog#713 note.
- **On every OS the platform layer can create a window after `Init` without a first one:**
  - macOS: `CreateWindow` makes the `NSWindow` and its Metal surface and does not order it front [platform_darwin.go:119-228](https://github.com/dvoyni/gogpu/blob/92d976d655fb366878fa120272e97093bf71cf1b/internal/platform/platform_darwin.go#L119-L228) [darwin/window.go:77-198](https://github.com/dvoyni/gogpu/blob/92d976d655fb366878fa120272e97093bf71cf1b/internal/platform/darwin/window.go#L77-L198).
  - Windows: `CreateWindowExW` with `WS_OVERLAPPEDWINDOW`, which lacks `WS_VISIBLE` [platform_windows.go:49](https://github.com/dvoyni/gogpu/blob/92d976d655fb366878fa120272e97093bf71cf1b/internal/platform/platform_windows.go#L49) [platform_windows.go:760-801](https://github.com/dvoyni/gogpu/blob/92d976d655fb366878fa120272e97093bf71cf1b/internal/platform/platform_windows.go#L760-L801).
  - X11: the first window initialises `x11Platform.inner` and every later one opens its own connection [platform_linux.go:239-283](https://github.com/dvoyni/gogpu/blob/92d976d655fb366878fa120272e97093bf71cf1b/internal/platform/platform_linux.go#L239-L283). `x11.Platform.Init` creates the window and does not map it [x11/platform.go:320-500](https://github.com/dvoyni/gogpu/blob/92d976d655fb366878fa120272e97093bf71cf1b/internal/platform/x11/platform.go#L320-L500); `Show` maps it [platform_linux.go:596-600](https://github.com/dvoyni/gogpu/blob/92d976d655fb366878fa120272e97093bf71cf1b/internal/platform/platform_linux.go#L596-L600).
  - Wayland: `Init` opens no connection [platform_linux.go:1029-1053](https://github.com/dvoyni/gogpu/blob/92d976d655fb366878fa120272e97093bf71cf1b/internal/platform/platform_linux.go#L1029-L1053); the first `CreateWindow` opens the primary connection, every later one its own [platform_linux.go:1058-1104](https://github.com/dvoyni/gogpu/blob/92d976d655fb366878fa120272e97093bf71cf1b/internal/platform/platform_linux.go#L1058-L1104).

### Close at once

platform's `Close` destroys a window and reports nothing; platform publishes `WindowClosedEvent` itself [platform/sockets.go:28-30](https://github.com/dvoyni/cog/blob/6581d1b0bab1c6b365fee243f0b3eb7c0554bd92/platform/sockets.go#L28-L30).

- **No public method destroys a window.** `Window.Close` only asks [window_manager.go:126-131](https://github.com/dvoyni/gogpu/blob/92d976d655fb366878fa120272e97093bf71cf1b/window_manager.go#L126-L131): the platform layer turns it into an `EventClose` on a later pump, which goes through the window's `onClose` [app.go:950-977](https://github.com/dvoyni/gogpu/blob/92d976d655fb366878fa120272e97093bf71cf1b/app.go#L950-L977) like a user's close. The method that destroys the surface and the platform window, `closeSecondaryWindow`, is private [window_manager.go:466-503](https://github.com/dvoyni/gogpu/blob/92d976d655fb366878fa120272e97093bf71cf1b/window_manager.go#L466-L503). **F5** makes it public.
- **The platform layers do not all destroy the window `closeSecondaryWindow` hands them:**
  - macOS: `darwinPlatformWindow.Destroy` is empty ("Destruction handled by platform.Destroy()") [platform_darwin.go:653-655](https://github.com/dvoyni/gogpu/blob/92d976d655fb366878fa120272e97093bf71cf1b/internal/platform/platform_darwin.go#L653-L655). The `NSWindow` stays in `p.windows`, is polled every pump, and is released only at shutdown [platform_darwin.go:304-337](https://github.com/dvoyni/gogpu/blob/92d976d655fb366878fa120272e97093bf71cf1b/internal/platform/platform_darwin.go#L304-L337). `darwin.Window.Destroy` exists to call [darwin/window.go:391-415](https://github.com/dvoyni/gogpu/blob/92d976d655fb366878fa120272e97093bf71cf1b/internal/platform/darwin/window.go#L391-L415). F5.
  - Windows: `Destroy` unlists and destroys the `HWND` [platform_windows.go:1232-1242](https://github.com/dvoyni/gogpu/blob/92d976d655fb366878fa120272e97093bf71cf1b/internal/platform/platform_windows.go#L1232-L1242). **Present.** `p.primary` keeps pointing at the first window after it is gone [platform_windows.go:895-913](https://github.com/dvoyni/gogpu/blob/92d976d655fb366878fa120272e97093bf71cf1b/internal/platform/platform_windows.go#L895-L913); only `WakeUp` [platform_windows.go:1399-1401](https://github.com/dvoyni/gogpu/blob/92d976d655fb366878fa120272e97093bf71cf1b/internal/platform/platform_windows.go#L1399-L1401) and the menu use it, and a `HostLoop` calls neither.
  - X11: `Destroy` returns at once for the first window [platform_linux.go:620-637](https://github.com/dvoyni/gogpu/blob/92d976d655fb366878fa120272e97093bf71cf1b/internal/platform/platform_linux.go#L620-L637). F5: give the first window the same treatment as the rest.
  - Wayland: `Destroy` returns at once for the first window, whose connection owns the seat, the clipboard and the cursor-shape manager [platform_linux.go:1002-1022](https://github.com/dvoyni/gogpu/blob/92d976d655fb366878fa120272e97093bf71cf1b/internal/platform/platform_linux.go#L1002-L1022). Missing; F10.

### Input in every window

- **macOS: events go to the first window.** `PollEvents` asks each window in turn, and the first one's `pollEvents` drains the whole `NSApp` queue through its own `handleEvent` [platform_darwin.go:231-242](https://github.com/dvoyni/gogpu/blob/92d976d655fb366878fa120272e97093bf71cf1b/internal/platform/platform_darwin.go#L231-L242) [platform_darwin.go:657-669](https://github.com/dvoyni/gogpu/blob/92d976d655fb366878fa120272e97093bf71cf1b/internal/platform/platform_darwin.go#L657-L669) [darwin/events.go:12-55](https://github.com/dvoyni/gogpu/blob/92d976d655fb366878fa120272e97093bf71cf1b/internal/platform/darwin/events.go#L12-L55). So under `HostLoop`, which only polls, every key, pointer and scroll event is stamped with the first window's ID, and pointer `Y` is flipped by the first window's height [platform_darwin.go:784-785](https://github.com/dvoyni/gogpu/blob/92d976d655fb366878fa120272e97093bf71cf1b/internal/platform/platform_darwin.go#L784-L785). `WaitEvents` already routes each event by `[NSEvent window]` [platform_darwin.go:252-292](https://github.com/dvoyni/gogpu/blob/92d976d655fb366878fa120272e97093bf71cf1b/internal/platform/platform_darwin.go#L252-L292). **F6** does the same in `PollEvents`.
- **Windows:** `wndProc` finds the window by `HWND` for every message [platform_windows.go:2733-2746](https://github.com/dvoyni/gogpu/blob/92d976d655fb366878fa120272e97093bf71cf1b/internal/platform/platform_windows.go#L2733-L2746). Present.
- **X11:** each window has its own connection, and `PollEvents` stamps each connection's events with its window [platform_linux.go:295-372](https://github.com/dvoyni/gogpu/blob/92d976d655fb366878fa120272e97093bf71cf1b/internal/platform/platform_linux.go#L295-L372). Present.
- **Wayland: later windows get no input.** "Input (keyboard/pointer) is NOT set up on secondary connections — it flows through the primary connection's seat" [platform_linux.go:1382-1385](https://github.com/dvoyni/gogpu/blob/92d976d655fb366878fa120272e97093bf71cf1b/internal/platform/platform_linux.go#L1382-L1385). A seat bound on one client connection never gets enter or focus events for surfaces of another, so no keyboard or pointer event reaches any window but the first. The input callbacks are all bound to the primary window [platform_linux.go:1682-1684](https://github.com/dvoyni/gogpu/blob/92d976d655fb366878fa120272e97093bf71cf1b/internal/platform/platform_linux.go#L1682-L1684), although a surface-to-window lookup exists [platform_linux.go:3598-3614](https://github.com/dvoyni/gogpu/blob/92d976d655fb366878fa120272e97093bf71cf1b/internal/platform/platform_linux.go#L3598-L3614). The code itself lists the per-window operations that act on the first window instead [platform_linux.go:641-648](https://github.com/dvoyni/gogpu/blob/92d976d655fb366878fa120272e97093bf71cf1b/internal/platform/platform_linux.go#L641-L648). Missing; F10.

### The user's close

`Window.SetOnClose(func() bool)` returning false keeps the window and is where the plugin reports `InputChangeCloseRequested`.

- On macOS the callback is the delegate's `windowShouldClose:` [window_manager.go:71-78](https://github.com/dvoyni/gogpu/blob/92d976d655fb366878fa120272e97093bf71cf1b/window_manager.go#L71-L78) [darwin/windowdelegate.go:39-56](https://github.com/dvoyni/gogpu/blob/92d976d655fb366878fa120272e97093bf71cf1b/internal/platform/darwin/windowdelegate.go#L39-L56).
- Elsewhere the close becomes an `EventClose` and `windowCloseEvent` asks the callback [app.go:950-958](https://github.com/dvoyni/gogpu/blob/92d976d655fb366878fa120272e97093bf71cf1b/app.go#L950-L958): Windows `WM_CLOSE` [platform_windows.go:2749-2752](https://github.com/dvoyni/gogpu/blob/92d976d655fb366878fa120272e97093bf71cf1b/internal/platform/platform_windows.go#L2749-L2752), X11 `WM_DELETE_WINDOW` [x11/platform.go:1015-1020](https://github.com/dvoyni/gogpu/blob/92d976d655fb366878fa120272e97093bf71cf1b/internal/platform/x11/platform.go#L1015-L1020), Wayland `xdg_toplevel.close` [platform_linux.go:2034-2041](https://github.com/dvoyni/gogpu/blob/92d976d655fb366878fa120272e97093bf71cf1b/internal/platform/platform_linux.go#L2034-L2041) [platform_linux.go:1541-1549](https://github.com/dvoyni/gogpu/blob/92d976d655fb366878fa120272e97093bf71cf1b/internal/platform/platform_linux.go#L1541-L1549).
- Present on every OS. Until F5, a close the plugin itself asks for comes back through the same callback, so the plugin's callback must let through the closes it asked for.

## `WindowEntry`

- **`Title`.** At `Open`: `platform.Config.Title` on every OS [darwin/window.go:124-132](https://github.com/dvoyni/gogpu/blob/92d976d655fb366878fa120272e97093bf71cf1b/internal/platform/darwin/window.go#L124-L132) [platform_windows.go:755-758](https://github.com/dvoyni/gogpu/blob/92d976d655fb366878fa120272e97093bf71cf1b/internal/platform/platform_windows.go#L755-L758) [platform_linux.go:240-241](https://github.com/dvoyni/gogpu/blob/92d976d655fb366878fa120272e97093bf71cf1b/internal/platform/platform_linux.go#L240-L241) [platform_linux.go:1207](https://github.com/dvoyni/gogpu/blob/92d976d655fb366878fa120272e97093bf71cf1b/internal/platform/platform_linux.go#L1207). ## Fork changes

Each is proposed upstream as its own PR, as cog#715 decided.

1. **F1, decided: start with no window, and one method that opens any window with its surface.** Found here: on macOS and Windows the method must apply `Config.Fullscreen` itself, which their platform layers ignore at creation, and must leave the window hidden until `Window.Show`. Small on top of the decided change.
2. **F2, decided: every window's resize applied at `BeginFrame`.**
3. **F3: per-window methods on `Window`:** `SetTitle`, `SetFullscreen`, `SetCursor`, `SetCursorMode`, `CursorMode`, `SetCursorPosition` and `ScaleFactor`, each forwarding to its `PlatformWindow`. The `App` versions act on the primary window only. Small.
4. **F4: per-window focus and scale reports.** `Window.SetOnFocus(func(bool))`, and the resize callback also on a scale-only change. On Wayland the scale change is detected at the pump, from the fractional-scale event, not in `PrepareFrame`. Small.
5. **F5: close any window at once.** A public `Window.Destroy` running `closeSecondaryWindow`'s body without asking `onClose`. macOS `darwinPlatformWindow.Destroy` unlists and releases the window; X11 destroys the first window's connection like any other. Small.
6. **F6: macOS `PollEvents` routes each `NSEvent` to its window,** as `WaitEvents` does. Small. It fixes key, text, pointer and scroll events of every window after the first.
7. **F7: macOS cursor and modifier fixes.** `CursorNone` hides once and the next shape unhides; Caps Lock joins the modifiers. Small.
8. **F8: the effective cursor mode on Windows, X11 and Wayland.** `CursorMode` answers Normal while the window lacks the focus or the clip, grab or lock is not in force; on Wayland from the locked, unlocked, confined and unconfined events. Small.
9. **F9: key labels and a layout-change report.** A call answering a key's unshifted character on the current layout, and an event when the layout changes. X11 and Wayland: small, from the xkb state already kept. Windows: new but small, `MapVirtualKeyExW` and `ToUnicodeEx` on the key's scancode, and `WM_INPUTLANGCHANGE`. macOS: new and larger, Carbon's `TISCopyCurrentKeyboardLayoutInputSource` and `UCKeyTranslate` through FFI, and the `kTISNotifySelectedKeyboardInputSourceChanged` distributed notification.
10. **F10: Wayland windows on one connection.** Every window a surface of the first connection, sharing its seat, its input routed by surface; every per-window operation on its own window; any window destroyable, the connection kept while the platform lives; `Hide` by committing a null buffer. Large: it reworks the secondary-connection design.
11. **F11: Wayland wheel scrolls as lines,** from `axis_discrete` or `axis_value120`. Small.
12. **F12: text the fork does not compose.** Dead keys on X11 and Wayland through `xkb_compose`: medium. IME, emoji picker and dictation commits through `NSTextInputClient` on macOS, XIM on X11 and `text-input-v3` on Wayland: large, and best taken with the IME composition effort platform left out of scope.

F1 to F8 and F11 are small. F9 is small on Linux and Windows and larger on macOS. F10 and F12 are the large ones; without F10, Wayland supports one window.
